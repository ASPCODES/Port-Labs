// The Vulnerability
The flaw is an integer overflow in the cart total. When you add items with a large quantity, the price (stored as a 32-bit signed integer in cents) overflows from a huge positive number → wraps around to a large negative number. By then adding cheap items to nudge the total just above $0 (but below your store credit), you can "buy" expensive items for free.
Attack flow:

Add the jacket with qty=99 repeatedly until total overflows to negative
Add cheap items (e.g. "Wrapping paper") to bring total to a small positive value ≤ your store credit ($100)
