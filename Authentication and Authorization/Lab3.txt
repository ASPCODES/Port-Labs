// Lab: Password reset broken logic


This lab's password reset functionality is vulnerable. To solve the lab, reset Carlos's password then log in and access his "My account" page.

Your credentials: wiener:peter

Victim's username: carlos 



// Solution:

Step 1 — Trigger a Reset for Your Own Account

Go to the "Forgot password" page.

Enter your username: wiener.

Check your email (the lab provides an email client) and copy the reset link/token



Step 2 — Intercept the Password Reset Request

Click the reset link from your email.

Open Burp Suite and turn on Intercept.

Fill in a new password and submit the form.

Burp will catch the POST request — it will look something like:


POST /forgot-password?temp-forgot-password-token=abc123

username=wiener&temp-forgot-password-token=abc123&new-password-1=test&new-password-2=test



Step 3 — Identify the Broken Logic

Notice that the username field is included in the POST body. The server is likely trusting this parameter to identify whose password to change, without verifying the token actually belongs to that user.



Step 4 — Manipulate the Request

In Burp, modify the username in the POST body:

username=carlos&temp-forgot-password-token=abc123&new-password-1=hacked123&new-password-2=hacked123

Keep the same token (your valid token), just change the username to carlos

Forward the request.



Step 5 — Log in as Carlos

Go to the login page.

Username: carlos.

Password: hacked123 (whatever you set).

Navigate to "My account" — lab solved!
